# Sound Design and Engineering

## Original synthesis

Every asset is generated by `src/generate_library.py`; no recordings or third-party samples are loaded by the generator.

### Tactile transients

Clicks and taps combine two or three exponentially decaying resonant modes with a very short high-passed noise transient. Varying modal frequency, decay and noise produces soft, crisp, rounded and mechanical variants.

### Presses and keyboard sounds

Low sine modes provide body. A shorter upper-frequency click provides tactile definition. Some variants include a separate release or deterministic sequence.

### Cards and transitions

Noise is FFT-filtered and shaped with a raised-sine envelope. Movement sounds are low-passed below 500 Hz where required by the Qiraa motion template. Stereo placement is subtle and peak normalization happens after channel gains.

### Notifications and success

Short inharmonic resonances keep notifications compact. Success sounds use small pitch sequences without speech or sampled instruments. Alerts use low, muted dissonance rather than loud buzzers.

## Technical decisions

- Sample rate: 48,000 Hz.
- PCM: signed 24-bit little-endian WAV.
- MP3: 192 kbps.
- Peak target: -15 dBFS across the complete file.
- DC removal and micro boundary fades prevent unintended edge clicks.
- SHA-256-derived seeds make every variation deterministic.
- The validator checks file count, uniqueness, duration, channels, bit depth, sample rate and peak level.

## Not a recording library

These are procedural UI sounds. They are not presented as recordings of physical devices, human foley, musical performances or proprietary operating-system sounds.
